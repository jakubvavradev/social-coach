# Growth & behaviour playbook

## Principles
1. **Content quality is 80 % of growth.** No engagement trick rescues a weak hook.
2. **Consistency beats bursts.** A steady rhythm the creator can sustain (e.g. 3–5 shorts a week, at the same time slot) beats 2 a day and then silence.
3. **Series beat one-offs.** A recurring format ("day N of building X") gives people a reason to follow, which is how views turn into follows.
4. **Own median, one variable.** Judge by trend over 5+ posts. Change one thing at a time.

## Posting routine
- **When:** at the profile's audience peak (each platform's analytics shows active hours once there are enough followers). Before that, early evening in the audience's main timezone is a safe default.
- **Before posting:** run the pre-flight review. Write a keyword caption (first line = hook + keyword). Use 3–5 specific hashtags, a cover frame without spoilers, and a check in the safe-zone preview.
- **First 30–60 min:** stay online. Reply to every comment, ideally with a question back, since that doubles the comment count and signals conversation. Pin the best comment, or your own CTA comment.
- **Cross-posting:** upload a clean native export to each platform, never another app's watermarked download. Adjust the caption and hashtags per platform.
- **Repurpose:** a long video gives 3–5 shorts. A good short gives a carousel or text post. A thread gives a short.

## Engagement (outbound)
- Spend 10–20 min a day on **real** comments under 5–10 peer and bigger accounts in the niche. Add an opinion, an extra fact, or a question; never "nice 🔥". Early comments on big accounts' fresh posts get seen.
- Reply to DMs and mentions. Duet or stitch peers (TikTok), and use remix or collab posts (IG Collab puts the post on both profiles).
- Collaborations: pitch creators of similar size with one concrete idea, not "let's collab".

## Profile (conversion)
- Name field and bio: who you are, what people get, and why they should follow (the series or promise). One link.
- Pin the 3 best posts that show the range of the account.
- Use consistent handle, avatar and banner on every platform.
- A grid or cover style that makes the topic readable at a glance.

## Comments and community
- Answer critics briefly and factually, or ignore them. Never argue at length. Delete or hide hate and spam, and use keyword filters.
- Turn good questions into the next video ("replying to @…" on TikTok).
- Never buy followers, views or likes. Fake audiences tank your engagement ratios and break the terms of service.

## Red lines (get accounts limited or banned)
- Follow/unfollow loops. Mass liking, following or commenting in bursts (new accounts are rate-limited hardest).
- Third-party automation or "growth" bots on IG or TikTok. Engagement pods. Identical copy-paste comments.
- Reposting others' content, other-app watermarks, using the same video on several of your own accounts.
- Misleading thumbnails or captions that promise something the video doesn't show (also hurts retention).
- Copyrighted music outside the platform's library. Undisclosed sponsorships. Undisclosed realistic AI content (use the platform's AI label when required).

## Safety and privacy (especially for minors)
- Never show your home location, school, city, daily routine, license plates, or documents.
- Get consent before showing other people's faces, especially other minors.
- Respect teen-account restrictions (DM limits, private defaults). Don't try to bypass them.
- Keep personal accounts and phone numbers out of public bios.

## Sustainability
Batch-film once a week, keep a backlog of 3–5 finished videos, and reuse formats. Burnout kills more channels than algorithms do.
