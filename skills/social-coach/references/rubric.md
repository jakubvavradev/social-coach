# Review rubric (100 points, 9 areas)

Score each area against its anchors. If a video falls between two anchors, take the lower one, so a score of 85+ really means "post it".
**Profile hard rules** (from `profile.md`): if a video breaks one, that area scores 0, whatever else is good in it.

## 1. Hook, 0–2 s (25)
Ask whether someone who doesn't follow you, scrolling with the sound off, would stop at frame 0.

| Pts | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 25 | Frame 0 shows the payoff or the most surprising thing. Motion starts within 0.5 s. Hook text is on screen at once. Sound hits at frame 0. The viewer knows what they will get. |
| 18 | Strong subject and text, but there is a ~0.5–1 s slow start, or a weak first frame that turns strong by 1 s. |
| 12 | The subject is clear but it is not surprising: a generic product shot, or text that is a label ("iPhone Duo") rather than a hook ("first time holding the iPhone Duo"). |
| 6 | Intro, logo, "hey guys", a black or blurry first frame, or the payoff only arrives after 3 s. |
| 0 | No reason to stop. |

Deductions: `leading_silence_s ≥ 0.4` −5 · a freeze starting before 2 s −8 · hook text inside a red safe-zone area −3.

## 2. Retention and pacing (20)
| Pts | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 20 | Something new every ≤2–3 s (a cut, zoom, text beat, reveal or sound accent). No dead middle. Length fits the idea, and nothing could be cut. |
| 14 | One soft spot of 2–4 s (a shot with no text or change). |
| 8 | Several flat stretches, or the video is ≥30 % longer than the idea needs. |
| 0 | Mostly static or repetitive, or a long video for a short idea. |

Use the flags as leads, then confirm them in the frames: a `freezes` entry is −5 each. A `longest_shot_s > 4` costs points only if the frames show no motion or text change. Duration over the profile max is −3, plus −1 for every 5 s beyond it.

## 3. On-screen text (10)
| Pts | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Hook text on frame 0, 3–6 words, high contrast, readable at phone size in under 1 s, inside the safe zone. Later text beats carry the story for muted viewers. Keywords for search appear on screen. |
| 6 | Hook text is there but long (>8 words), small, low-contrast, or partly covered by UI. Or the middle has no text. |
| 3 | Text only at the end, or burned captions that cover the subject. |
| 0 | No text at all on a video that needs it. |

## 4. Payoff and loop (10)
| Pts | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Delivers what the hook promised, at the end (not the start), and the end runs back into frame 0 (seam, matching motion, or a sentence that completes at the start). |
| 6 | Pays off, but the ending is a hard stop (black, logo, outro card). |
| 3 | The payoff is weaker than the promise (bait). |
| 0 | No payoff. |
`loop_ssim > 0.6` suggests a visual loop. A low value is fine if the loop is narrative.

## 5. Shareability (10)
Would someone DM this to a friend? Sends and shares are top signals on IG and TikTok.
10 = clear send trigger (surprise, "you need to see this", useful/saveable, relatable, a debate starter) · 5 = interesting but personal · 0 = no reason to share.

## 6. Audio (10)
| Pts | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 10 | Music or voice from frame 0, about −14 LUFS (±2.5), true peak ≤ −1 dBFS, voice clearly above the music, cuts or beats synced, the track fits the mood. Complies with profile music rules (credit, no repeats). |
| 6 | Fine but not synced, or slightly too quiet or loud. |
| 3 | Music fights the voice, or it clips. |
| 0 | Silent, or breaks a profile music rule. |

## 7. CTA (5)
5 = one CTA with stakes: an opinion question with two sides ("would you pay $1,999 / €1,849?"), a choice, or a keyword comment. It must be answerable in under 3 words · 2 = generic ("follow for more", "like & subscribe") · 0 = none, or several competing CTAs.

## 8. Technical (5)
5 = 1080×1920 9:16, ≥30 fps, sharp, well exposed, no black frames, no foreign watermark · −2 for each issue. Size limits of the upload path are in `platforms.md`.

## 9. Originality (5)
5 = own footage, own idea, or a trend with an obvious personal twist · 2 = a trend format with no twist · 0 = reposted or aggregated content, or another app's watermark (this makes it ineligible for recommendations on IG and TikTok).

## Verdict
**85+ post it · 70–84 post after the top-3 fixes · <70 re-cut first.** Any profile hard-rule violation means "PŘESTŘIHNOUT", whatever the score.

## Ranking the fixes
Rank by expected impact: hook > retention > text > audio > everything else. A fix must be executable without another conversation: give the timestamp, what to change, and the exact text, cut, or track.
