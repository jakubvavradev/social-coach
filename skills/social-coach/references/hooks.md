# Hooks: the first 2 seconds

Viewers decide in roughly 1–2 s. Paid-ad data (Billo, 88k Meta ads, H1 2026) puts the average 3-s hook rate at 25 % and calls 30 %+ strong. Organic content is less studied, but the shape is the same: most of the loss happens at second 0–1.

## A hook has three layers. All must agree.
1. **Visual:** the first frame is the most interesting frame. Start mid-action, at the result, or at the strangest moment.
2. **Text:** 3–6 words that make a promise or open a gap. Readable in under 1 s, sitting in the upper-middle safe area.
3. **Audio:** sound at frame 0 (a beat drop, the first word, an SFX). No silent lead-in.

## Hook types (pick one per video, and vary them across variants)
| Type | Mechanism | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Result-first | show the payoff, then how | "I built my own TV OS" + the TV turning on |
| Curiosity gap | promise information, withhold it | "nobody tells you this about ___" |
| Challenge / stakes | a risk to the creator | "if this robot crashes, I lose the race" |
| Contrarian | go against a belief | "___ is a waste of money. Here's why" |
| Number / list | promise a bounded payoff | "3 things AI built for me this week" |
| Before → after | transformation | "old launcher vs mine" |
| POV / relatable | the viewer sees themselves | "POV: you asked AI to fix one bug" |
| First / novelty | a new thing, first look | "first time holding the ___" |
| Question with sides | invite a quick opinion | "would you pay $X for this?" |
| Mistake / fail | humans love fails | "I wasted 2 weeks because of this" |
| Speed / time | compression | "from zero to working app in 1 day" |
| Comparison | A vs B | "$20 robot vs $200 robot" |
| Secret / insider | exclusive access | "this is what a robotics competition looks like from the pit" |
| Pattern interrupt | unexpected visual or sound | object flying into frame, sudden zoom |

## Text rules
- Sentence case or all lowercase. Use a bold sans font with a stroke or box behind it for contrast.
- Upper-middle placement (y ≈ 250–700 px at 1080×1920), never under the caption area or the right-hand icons.
- One idea per text beat. Change the text at every beat so muted viewers can follow.
- Include the search keyword (product name, topic). TikTok and IG index on-screen text.

## Anti-hooks (automatic Hook ≤ 6)
Intro, logo animation, "hi guys / welcome back", a slow fade from black, a title card, an establishing shot of nothing, an explanation before the visual, or "part 1" with no stakes.

## Writing 3 variants
Use three **different types** from the table, not three phrasings of one idea. Example for a foldable phone: Result-first "it folds in half 🤯" · Question "would you pay $2,000?" · First/novelty "first time holding the iPhone Duo".
