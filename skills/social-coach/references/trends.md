# Trend scouting

Goal: 3–5 ideas the creator can **film this week**. Each one has evidence, is caught at the right stage, and carries the creator's own twist. A trend without a twist is a copy, and copies lose to the original and risk the unoriginal-content filters.

## Sources (go through them in this order, read-only)
| # | Source | What to pull | Freshness |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TikTok Creative Center** → Trends (ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) | Songs, Hashtags, Creators, Videos. Set the region to the profile's audience countries and the period to 7 days. Prefer **Breakout / rising** over **Popular** (already peaked). | daily |
| 2 | **TikTok / IG search** for niche keywords | Sort by recent. Look for formats repeated by unrelated creators within 7 days. | live |
| 3 | **IG Reels audio pages** | A "↗ trending" arrow plus a low use count (<10–20k) means early. | live |
| 4 | **YouTube Shorts search**, filtered to Shorts + this week | **Outliers**: views ≫ subscribers (≥10×). That means the format works, not the creator's fame. | live |
| 5 | **Google Trends** (trends.google.com) | Topic spikes over 7 days. Switch the search type to *YouTube Search*. Compare with 2–3 related terms. | hourly |
| 6 | **Tech/news feeds** for the niche (The Verge, Hacker News front page, X lists, Reddit r/<niche> top/day) | Launches and controversies in the last 48 h form the newsjacking window. | hours |
| 7 | **Peer accounts** (5–10 creators the same size in the niche) | Posts from the last 7 days that beat that creator's own median by ≥3×. | weekly |
| 8 | **Calendar** | Holidays, product launch dates, competitions, school periods, platform events. | plan ahead |

Use WebSearch or a fetch/scrape tool for public pages. Use a browser for logged-in feeds, but only ever look. Never like, follow, comment, post, or run automation on IG or TikTok while researching.

## Stage model
| Stage | Signals | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Rising** | <1–2 weeks old, use count growing fast, few big creators on it yet | Film within 48 h. Best ROI. |
| **Peak** | everywhere, big accounts using it, meme saturation | Only with a strong twist, and fast. |
| **Dying** | parodies of the trend, use count flat, comments say "this again" | Skip, unless you flip it ironically. |
| **Evergreen format** | the structure keeps working for months (before/after, POV, rate my X, $ vs $$) | Use anytime, refreshed with your content. |

Typical lifetimes [3rd-party]: sounds ~1–3 weeks, meme formats 2–6 weeks, news 24–72 h, evergreen formats months to years.

## Twist framework
For each trend ask: **which of my pillars can wear this format?**
- Map: trend format → pillar → the creator's real asset (a project, footage, data).
- Ask whether it could only come from this creator. If anyone could post it, sharpen the twist.
- Keep the trend's recognisable element (the sound, the text pattern, the structure) so the audience recognises it, and swap the subject for your own.

## Trend card (output format)
```
### 1. <trend name>  — RISING
Důkaz: <link> · <čísla: počet použití/views> · <datum ≤7 dní>
Co to je: <1 věta, jak formát funguje>
Twist pro nás: <pilíř> → <konkrétní záběr z našeho materiálu>
Hook text: "<≤6 slov>"
Zvuk: <trend sound / NCS/CC track + credit; dodrž pravidla profilu>
Natočit do: <datum> · Náročnost: S/M/L
```
End with **"Doporučení: začni č. X, protože …"**.

## Trend log
Append each scout run to `~/.claude/social-coach/trends-log.md` (date, trends, chosen one, result after 7 days). Over time this shows which trend types actually work for this account.

## Music note
On TikTok and IG, a trending sound added **in-app at upload** carries the platform's license and the trend's discovery boost. For an exported video with baked-in music, use properly licensed CC/NCS tracks and credit them. Check the profile's music rules (for example "never repeat a track").
